Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar browser technologies may be used when you visit Gladihoppers Games. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage and identifiers used to remember settings, understand website traffic, maintain game progress, or support embedded content.

How They May Be Used

  • Essential storage: supports basic website or embedded-game functionality.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics may collect information about visits, pages viewed, device type, and referral sources so website usage can be understood.
  • Game storage: an embedded game may use cookies or local storage for preferences and progress.
  • Third-party content: services such as the embedded game and Google Fonts may receive technical request information under their own policies.

Managing Cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers also allow you to restrict third-party cookies or clear site data for a specific domain. Blocking storage may prevent game progress, preferences, or parts of embedded content from working correctly.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when website tools or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page. Questions can be submitted through our Contact Us page.
