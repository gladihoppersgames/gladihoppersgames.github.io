About Us

Gladihoppers Games is a browser-gaming website created to make entertaining online games easy to discover and play. Our goal is to organize accessible experiences across action, arcade, adventure, cars, sports, horror, puzzles, and two-player categories without requiring visitors to install additional software.

What We Offer

We focus on games that can run directly in a modern browser on supported desktop and mobile devices. Category pages provide clear descriptions and will be expanded with carefully organized game collections over time. Our main Gladihoppers page also offers convenient access to the embedded game and information for new players.

Independent Website

Gladihoppers Games is an independent website. Game names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. When a game is embedded or provided by a third party, that provider remains responsible for its game content, availability, and associated services.

Our Approach

We aim to keep navigation simple, information useful, and access transparent. If you notice an incorrect description, a technical problem, or content that should be reviewed, please use our Contact Us page.
